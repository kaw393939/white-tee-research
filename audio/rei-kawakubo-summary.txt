Rei Kawakubo is a reference for questioning what clothing can be.

The Japanese designer founded Comme des Garçons. Her work explores boundaries between clothing, the body, and art. The Metropolitan Museum of Art devoted its twenty-seventeen exhibition to that approach.

For our plain white T-shirt, the lesson is to question an assumption before adding decoration. Must the shirt be fitted? Must it be symmetrical? Must it be presented as an undershirt? These questions can help us find a distinct point of view while we decide what customers actually need.

On the website, experiment with image crops or editorial layouts, but keep price, sizing, and buying controls easy to find. An unusual presentation should still be understandable.

Try making two mood boards for the same tee: one quiet and one experimental. Ask classmates what each suggests about the wearer and price. These are our applications of Kawakubo's work, not recommendations from her.
