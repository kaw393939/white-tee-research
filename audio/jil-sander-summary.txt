Jil Sander is a reference for making a simple product feel carefully considered.

The German designer established her company and first collection in nineteen sixty-eight. Her work combines minimalism with technical and material experimentation. We are studying her approach as a designer, rather than treating every later collection from her namesake label as her own work.

For our plain white T-shirt, the useful lesson is precision. Pay attention to the neckline, fabric weight, stitching, and proportions. A restrained design still needs details that justify a closer look.

On the website, use clear product photographs, readable type, and specific facts about the shirt. Help shoppers understand quality instead of relying on the word premium.

Try sketching one product page with a full-shirt photograph, a fabric close-up, and three concrete facts. Ask a classmate what explains the price. These are our applications of Sander's work, not recommendations or an endorsement from her.
