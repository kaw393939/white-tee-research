# Our brand

We’re building a brand around **plain white T-shirts**. The audience, price, fit, name, and style are still open.

After competitor research, we’ll develop:

1. **Customer persona:** who we serve and what they need.
2. **Brand direction:** positioning, offer, voice, and visual style.
3. **Website plan:** shopping experience, content, and coding plan.

Brand archetypes, Cialdini’s persuasion principles, and modern or postmodern design are ideas to explore—not decisions yet.

## Designers to learn from

Creative references to explore—not a chosen direction or a ranking. The examples below are ideas for our project.

| Designer | What we can learn | Example for our white tee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Jil Sander](https://www.thonet.de/fileadmin/media/08-company/Press_releases/JS_._THONET/3_Thonet_Designer_profile_Jil_Sander_EN.pdf) | Make simplicity feel intentional through precision and material quality. | Show the neckline, fabric texture, and fit with clear photography and restrained typography. |
| [Christophe Lemaire](https://www.lemaire.fr/pages/about-lemaire), working with Sarah-Linh Tran | Build around everyday use, movement, and proportions. | Show the tee worn alone and layered; explain its length, drape, and comfort in practical terms. |
| [Rei Kawakubo](https://www.metmuseum.org/ko/press-releases/rei-kawakubo-2016-exhibitions) | Question conventions and create a distinctive point of view. | Test unexpected image crops or editorial layouts while keeping sizing, price, and checkout easy to find. |

**Starting point:** study Sander and Lemaire for the product and everyday experience; use Kawakubo to explore a more experimental identity. Validate the direction through research before committing.
