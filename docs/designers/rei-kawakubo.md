## Brief bio

Rei Kawakubo is a Japanese designer and the founder of Comme des Garçons. Her work questions familiar boundaries between clothing, the body, and art. The Metropolitan Museum of Art devoted its 2017 exhibition, *Rei Kawakubo/Comme des Garçons: Art of the In-Between*, to that approach. [The Met’s exhibition](https://www.metmuseum.org/exhibitions/listings/2017/rei-kawakubo).

## Apply it to our white tee

- **Product:** question one assumption—must a white tee be fitted, symmetrical, or styled as an undershirt?
- **Website:** test unexpected image crops or editorial compositions while keeping price, size, and buying controls clear.
- **Brand:** develop one recognizable idea instead of adding decoration everywhere.

These are our proposed applications, not recommendations made by Kawakubo.

**Try this:** make two mood boards for the same plain tee: quiet and experimental. Ask classmates what each suggests about the wearer and price.
