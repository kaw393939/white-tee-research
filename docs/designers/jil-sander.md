**Study for:** restraint, material quality, and precision.

## Brief bio

Jil Sander is a German fashion designer who established her company and first collection in 1968. Her work combines minimalism with technical and material experimentation. We’re studying the founder’s design approach, rather than attributing every later collection from the Jil Sander label to her. [Biography: Thonet](https://www.thonet.de/fileadmin/media/08-company/Press_releases/JS_._THONET/3_Thonet_Designer_profile_Jil_Sander_EN.pdf).

**[View portraits of Jil Sander](https://www.museumangewandtekunst.de/en/home/print/?id=322&images=0)** — Museum Angewandte Kunst’s exhibition page includes photographs credited to Peter Lindbergh. Portraits are linked at their source; no reusable portrait has been verified for this page.

## Apply it to our white tee

- **Product:** make neckline shape, fabric weight, and stitching worth a closer look.
- **Website:** use uncluttered product photography and readable typography; show details rather than relying on “premium” claims.
- **Brand:** communicate confidence through consistency and restraint.

These are our proposed applications, not recommendations made by Sander.

**Try this:** sketch one product page with a full-shirt photo, a fabric close-up, and three specific product facts. Ask a classmate what justifies the price.
