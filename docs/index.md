## First three competitors

| Brand | Single white tee | What makes the offer different |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ASKET](/competitors/asket) | $65 USD | Detailed specifications and three lengths |
| [Son of a Tailor](/competitors/son-of-a-tailor) | $75 USD | Custom fit and made-to-order production |
| [True Classic](/competitors/true-classic) | $34.99 regular; $29.99 sale | Fit-led messaging, bundles, and promotions |

Observed November 9, 2026. These are three useful starting benchmarks, not the whole market. All currently sell more than one product type. Prices exclude unverified taxes or fees; see each profile for sources, review limits, and an audio brief.

[Compare their archetypes and persuasion techniques](/research/brand-strategy) — a short guide with evidence and one exercise to try.

## What we’re learning

- **Fit takes different forms:** length options at ASKET, customization at Son of a Tailor, and body-shape benefits at True Classic.
- **Proof matters:** specifications, production explanations, and customer reviews support different promises. Marketing claims still need validation.
- **White is a specific product problem:** Son of a Tailor discloses slight translucency. Opacity and wash performance deserve physical tests across all three.

These are initial observations from the linked profiles, not a decision about our brand. **Next:** read independent and critical reviews, then test sample shirts.
