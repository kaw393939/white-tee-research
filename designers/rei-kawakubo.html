Rei Kawakubo ​

Study for: challenging conventions and building a distinctive point of view.

Brief bio ​

Rei Kawakubo is a Japanese designer and the founder of Comme des Garçons. Her work questions familiar boundaries between clothing, the body, and art. The Metropolitan Museum of Art devoted its 2017 exhibition, Rei Kawakubo/Comme des Garçons: Art of the In-Between, to that approach. The Met’s exhibition.

View a portrait of Rei Kawakubo — Tommy Ton’s photograph accompanies GQ’s account of making the portrait. The photograph is linked at its source; no reuse license has been verified here.

Apply it to our white tee ​

  • Product: question one assumption—must a white tee be fitted, symmetrical, or styled as an undershirt?
  • Website: test unexpected image crops or editorial compositions while keeping price, size, and buying controls clear.
  • Brand: develop one recognizable idea instead of adding decoration everywhere.

These are our proposed applications, not recommendations made by Kawakubo.

Try this: make two mood boards for the same plain tee: quiet and experimental. Ask classmates what each suggests about the wearer and price.
