Interpretation guardrails ​

Compare tax and shipping consistently. Keep promotional prices in profiles. Missing specifications are not evidence of poor quality. Brand claims about softness, opacity, sustainability, or longevity need independent validation before becoming conclusions.

Landscape synthesis ​

Populate after profiles are reviewed:

  • Observed price bands within a market: Not researched.
  • Recurring positioning themes: Not researched.
  • Product and service tradeoffs: Not researched.
